**Theme - Purgatory**

- **Premise:** You died in the first battle of a great war, but your soul became trapped in a twisted medieval limbo. To attain eternal rest, you must hunt and liberate the spirits of your former brothers-in-arms, who went mad and became guardians of the abyss.
    

**Classes**

**Iron Penitent**

- **Concept:** A knight bent under the weight of a broken vow, bound to armor corroded by sacred chains.
    
- **Special Ability:** _Martyrdom of the Flesh_. Converts incoming physical damage into a strength bonus for the next attack.
    
- **Mechanics:** High physical resistance and a large health pool, but loses sanity faster when targeted by spiritual spells.
    

**Shadow Reaper**

- **Concept:** The soul of a mercenary who refuses to forget his final contract, wielding blades of ethereal energy.
    
- **Special Ability:** _Essence Sever_. Ignores enemy physical armor, dealing damage directly to the target's soul.
    
- **Mechanics:** High speed and critical hit rate, but possesses the lowest health bar in the roster.
    

**Ash Bearer**

- **Concept:** A former war priest who channels the mortal remains of fallen comrades to erect shields and invoke grey flames.
    
- **Special Ability:** _Shroud of Oblivion_. Creates a fog of ashes that reduces enemy accuracy and restores group sanity.
    
- **Mechanics:** Support and crowd control focused class, capable of heavily manipulating the battlefield state.
    

**Blind Inquisitor**

- **Concept:** A combatant who gouged out his own sight to avoid succumbing to the hallucinations and distortions of purgatory.
    
- **Special Ability:** _Abyssal Perception_. Uncovers hidden weaknesses of monsters and bosses, increasing overall team damage against the target.
    
- **Mechanics:** Completely immune to visual effects and low-sanity hallucinations, but relies on abilities to ensure physical attacks hit.
    

**Consumables**

- **Ether Tear:** Distillation of purified souls. Restores 30 Sanity points and dispels temporary hallucinations.
    
- **Cold Ash Elixir:** A bitter brew that stanches wounds. Restores 40 Health points, but reduces speed on the next turn by 10%.
    
- **Barbed Soul Shard:** A crystallized relic. Increases spiritual damage by 25% for 3 turns, but drains 5 Health points upon consumption.
    

**Relics and Equipment**

- **Penitent's Chain:** A heavy iron ring. Grants +15 Defense when Sanity drops below 30%.
    
- **Obsidian Eye:** A darkened gem that reflects the truth. Reveals enemy weak spots and negates fog ambushes.
    
- **Corroded Brass Bell:** A profane instrument. When rung, draws all enemy aggression to the user and grants a shield worth 20% of maximum Health.
    

**Key Items**

- **Seal of the Forgotten:** A worn bronze symbol required to break the chains sealing access to the deeper levels of Purgatory.
    

**Quests**

**1. Rescue of the Echo (Main)**

- **Context:** The driven-mad spirit of your former Commander wanders through the Valley of Whispers, unable to accept final defeat in war.
    
- **Objective:** Find the Commander in the depths of the dungeon, defeat his corrupted form, and free his soul.
    
- **Reward:** _Seal of the Forgotten_ (Key Item) + 150 Experience Points.
    

**2. The Broken Vow (Side)**

- **Context:** Three guardian statues hold the chains of your guilt in Purgatory. Destroying them will weaken the zone's seal.
    
- **Objective:** Locate and purify the 3 Altars of Penitence scattered across the map.
    
- **Reward:** _Penitent's Chain_ (Equipment) + Full Sanity Restoration.
    

**3. The Unquenchable Flame (Hunt / Gathering)**

- **Context:** The rest campfires have gone out. Without light, hallucinations consume anyone who attempts to rest.
    
- **Objective:** Defeat Ash Wraiths and collect 5 _Cold Ash Essences_.
    
- **Reward:** _Ether Tear_ (x2) + Chapter Save Point Access.
    

**Adversaries (Enemies)**

- **Wraith of Guilt**
    
    - **Concept:** An ethereal entity born from unresolved remorse of lost souls.
        
    - **Combat Style:** Fast and elusive. Attacks the player's mind directly, draining **Sanity** before inflicting physical damage.
        
    - **Ability:** _Abyssal Whisper_ (Deals 15 Mental Damage and reduces player accuracy for 2 turns).
        
- **Petrified Warrior**
    
    - **Concept:** A veteran soldier whose armor fused with the corroded stone and metal of Purgatory.
        
    - **Combat Style:** Slow, but sports nearly impenetrable physical defense.
        
    - **Ability:** _Ground Slam_ (Pulls the player in and deals heavy Physical Damage, ignoring a portion of armor).
        
- **Corrupted Commander (Zone Boss)**
    
    - **Concept:** The former leader of your army. His refusal to accept defeat transformed him into a deformed monstrosity that commands the shadows.
        
    - **Combat Style:** Two phases. Uses heavy sword strikes in the first phase; unleashes his aura in the second, dealing continuous area-of-effect damage.
        
    - **Ability:** _War Lament_ (Increases his own attack power and forces the player to skip 1 turn due to shock).
        

**NPCs (Non-Player Characters)**

- **The Blind Occultist**
    
    - **Role:** Abyssal Merchant.
        
    - **Function:** Rejects coins. Exchanges rare items and consumables for the player's maximum **Sanity** or _Soul Shards_.
        
- **The Keeper of Grief**
    
    - **Role:** Spiritual Guide and Rest Point.
        
    - **Function:** Offers main quests, reveals fragments of the player's past, and restores Health and Sanity in exchange for relics.
        
- **The Broken Blacksmith**
    
    - **Role:** Equipment Forger.
        
    - **Function:** A soul chained to a burning anvil. Upgrades player weapons and armor using purified ashes collected from enemies.
